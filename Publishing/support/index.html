Frequently Asked Questions

Does the App collect my data?
No. All game data is stored locally on your device. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I reset my progress?
Open Settings in the App, then tap "Reset All Stats". This permanently erases all career stats, achievements, and past game history.

Does the App need an internet connection?
No. The App works fully offline.

How does Dynasty Mode work?
When you win the league, you can continue with the same team next season and make 1–5 transfers between seasons.

How do I play penalties?
If "Playable Penalties" is enabled in Settings, you take shootouts yourself by picking a spot on the goal. 10 of 15 squares are goals; the rest are saved.

How many players can I transfer midseason?
With "Midseason Transfers" enabled, you can make 1–3 transfers during a season.
